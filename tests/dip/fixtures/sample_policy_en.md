# Corporate Data Handling Policy

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Article 1: Scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors of the organization.
All personnel must comply with this policy when handling company data.

### Article 2: Definitions
(1) "Confidential data" means any information classified as restricted or above.
(2) "Data handler" refers to any person authorized to access company data systems.

## Chapter 2: Obligations

### Article 3: Data Classification
(1) All data must be classified according to the organization's classification scheme.
(2) The data owner is responsible for assigning the appropriate classification level.
(3) Unclassified data shall be treated as internal by default.

### Article 4: Access Control
(1) Employees must not access data beyond their authorization level.
(2) Access credentials must not be shared with any third party.
(3) The IT department may revoke access at any time if a security concern arises.

### Article 5: Data Retention
(1) Personal data must be deleted within 30 days after the purpose of processing has ended.
(2) Financial records must be retained for a minimum of 7 years.
(3) This article does not apply to anonymized data.

## Chapter 3: Prohibitions

### Article 6: Prohibited Actions
(1) Employees are prohibited from transferring confidential data to external systems without authorization.
(2) The use of personal devices for processing confidential data is forbidden unless explicitly approved by the CISO.
(3) It is prohibited to bypass security controls or disable audit logging.

## Chapter 4: Exceptions

### Article 7: Emergency Access
(1) Notwithstanding Article 4, emergency access may be granted by the CISO
in case of a critical security incident.
(2) Emergency access must be documented and reviewed within 24 hours.
